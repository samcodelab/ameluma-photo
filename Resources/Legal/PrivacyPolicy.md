# Ameluma Photo Privacy Policy

Broad Frame Studio develops Ameluma Photo. Your photographs are processed on your device using Apple frameworks. Ameluma Photo has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, developer-operated cloud service or automatic image uploads. Broad Frame Studio does not receive your photographs, editing settings or recognition results through the app.

## What stays on your device

Imported photographs are copied into Ameluma Photo's local application storage so editing can continue after closing the app. Originals are not modified. Local storage also contains project instructions, source names and references, image classification labels, framing proposals, favourites, presets, processing history, unfinished jobs and completed outputs. Mac automation settings include permissions for folders you explicitly select. These records are used to restore editing, find images, avoid repeated processing and recover unfinished work.

Saved data remains until removed from the device. Creating a new project preserves the earlier project; it does not erase its photographs. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app retains data. Files exported elsewhere, Photos additions, shared copies and operating-system backups remain under your control. On Mac, deleting the application alone may leave application-support data; use the storage location shown in Help & Privacy to manage local files after quitting Ameluma Photo. Deleting stored sources can make projects unavailable. No secure-erasure guarantee is made.

## Browser image drops on Mac

Dragging a browser image into Ameluma Photo first uses image bytes provided by the browser, if available. If the drop supplies only an HTTPS image address, Ameluma Photo requests that address to download the image. The website and its image-hosting providers receive the normal network request, including your IP address and any information contained in the address you dragged. Browser cookies, stored login credentials and local photographs are not sent by Ameluma Photo. There is no developer-operated proxy, analytics or upload service. Downloaded images are validated and processed locally. This optional acquisition step needs a network connection; local editing and file imports work offline. Downloads are limited to 25 MB, may follow up to five HTTPS redirects and can be cancelled. Sites requiring login may require saving the image in the browser first.

## Permissions and system services

Photos selection uses Apple's item-based Photos picker and does not require access to your entire library. Save to Photos requests permission only to add finished images. You can revoke permissions in system Settings. Files and Finder panels grant access only to the files or folders you choose. Mac automation folders can be changed or their workflows paused/deleted in Automations; deleting a workflow leaves originals and completed files intact.

Apple-managed Photos, Files, backups and native sharing may download cloud-only selections, synchronise your chosen destinations or deliver files to an app or service you select. Those actions follow your choices and system settings and are outside Ameluma Photo's control. Broad Frame Studio does not operate those services. Local sources and local destinations can be processed offline.

## Privacy editing and metadata

Vision analysis runs on-device. Search and auto-framing can make mistakes. Privacy Review supports temporary manual flags. Optional Apple Sensitive Content Analysis runs locally only when the required capability and system policy permit it; unavailable analysis is identified clearly. Findings are held in memory, not saved to projects or logged. Detection can miss sensitive content and does not certify that an image is safe. Masked final exports contain flattened edited pixels and omit copied supported source metadata and original thumbnails. Blur, pixelation and metadata removal are not guarantees of concealment or anonymity. Inspect the final image before sharing; opaque covers and cropping are preferable when stronger concealment is needed.

Private Editing Mode avoids the app's thumbnail memory cache and can conceal its workspace. It does not encrypt files, prevent operating-system screenshots/backups or securely delete data. Saved photographs, projects and recovery outputs remain on disk. Editable project files may disclose source references and reversible edits; share processed images when you intend to conceal content.

## Policy and support

This policy describes Ameluma Photo's current behaviour. The public policy and support links, when configured, are available in Help & Privacy. Opening those links uses your system browser; no photographs or project data are attached. If you contact support, send only the information you choose to disclose.
