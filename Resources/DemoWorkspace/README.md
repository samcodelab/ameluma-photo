# Ameluma Photo demo photographs

30 individual AI-generated photographic images, created on 16–17 October 2026 using OpenAI's built-in image_gen tool (`photorealistic-natural`; landscape companion: `identity-preserve`). No external photograph, contact sheet, competitor artwork or stock collection was used as a source. The landscape companion uses our own generated vertical hero as its reference. The people and scenes are synthetic; locations are evocative rather than verified depictions of real places. No real-person identity or model release is asserted.

## Intended usage

These assets were created for Ameluma Photo's bundled Demo Workspace, editing tests, tutorials, website and App Store screenshots. Keep this provenance alongside redistributed source assets. This record documents creation and intended usage; it does not assert exclusive copyright, a third-party stock licence or a real-person release. Marketing should describe them as sample/generated photos when that distinction matters. Never imply they were captured or generated by Ameluma's on-device editing tools.

`30-lorikeet-hero-clean.jpg` is the main landscape hero, with a central bird and uncluttered forest background. The first bird image, `28-rainbow-bird-hero.jpg`, remains as an alternate: an original generated wildlife photograph of a colourful rainbow lorikeet. `29-golden-light-portrait.jpg` is a polished, beautifully lit portrait of a fictional Black woman. Both coastal portraits (`27-coastal-hero-landscape.jpg` and `01-coastal-hero.jpg`) remain available. The wildlife image is inspired by high-quality field photography; it is not a real wildlife sighting or an image published or endorsed by National Geographic. Photos 02–21 cover landscapes, pets, food, travel, architecture and night scenes. Photos 22–26 intentionally demonstrate underexposure, warm white balance, a tilted horizon, flat colour/contrast and loose subject framing. The collection supports different crop ratios and batch workflows rather than a permanent photo-library experience.

## Files and reproducibility

- `Photos/`: 30 independent JPEG originals for the demo, all encoded once from separate generated PNGs at JPEG quality 96 with full chroma resolution. Native generation dimensions were retained: 1024×1536, 1536×1024 or 1254×1254. No upscaling or contact-sheet extraction.
- `manifest.json`: ordered filenames, hero designation, titles, categories, suggested exercises, dimensions, packaged SHA-256 and original generated-PNG SHA-256.
- `generation-prompts.json`: the full per-image prompt set and tool attribution. Regeneration is stochastic and will not reproduce identical images.
- Original generation PNGs remain in the generating chat's default `/Users/sam/.codex/generated_images/01a143a6-ec3c-7681-8636-796f1722c535` output folder. Packaged JPEGs do not preserve the generator PNG's embedded content-credentials container; provenance is explicitly retained here instead.

These are 8-bit rendered images, not camera RAW files. They test ordinary editing, crop, export and screenshot presentation, not sensor noise, RAW recovery, wide-gamut/HDR accuracy, EXIF/GPS removal, transparency, damaged files or very large input performance. Use dedicated fixtures for those cases. A photo with a simulated exposure problem cannot provide genuine RAW highlight/shadow latitude.

The central lorikeet hero was generated by simplifying and recomposing an unselected generated bird-and-blossom concept. All references were our own generated images. The complete final edit prompt and reference path are recorded in generation-prompts.json.
